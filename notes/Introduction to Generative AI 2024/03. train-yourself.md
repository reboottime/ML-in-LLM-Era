# Train Yourself to be a better commander

Treat it like a human being and behave like a good commander. Explain clearly: provide a clear & concrete goal, command, and context.

My(Kate's) Understanding

- Provide computational thinking related keywords to LLM
- Set clear promise (context)
- In context learning

## mantras to LLMs

- > Let's think step by step
  - This mantra also works for GPT 4 on reading image content
  - Asking model to explain its result also helps
- > Answer by starting with analysis
- > Emotional Blackmail. This is very important to [xx]

![.](./screenshots/emotional-blackmail.jpg)

You can find more mantras at article - [Principled instructions are all you need for questioning](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.16171)

> being polite doesn't make LLMs more effective
> Employ affirmative directives such as 'do, while steering clear of
negative language like 'don't'.
> Add "I'm going to tip $xxx for a better solution"
> Incorporate the following phrases: `You will be penalized`
> `Ensure that your answer is  unbiased and avoids relying on sterotypes`

Kate's version:

> Do not take actions until you have >= 95% confidence to what I'm talking about. Before that, you ask clarification questions
